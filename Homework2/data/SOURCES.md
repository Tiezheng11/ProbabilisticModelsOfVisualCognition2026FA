# Sources of the data files

- `attention/2.jpg`, `attention/5.jpg`: images 35010 and 97017 (training set) of the Berkeley Segmentation Dataset BSDS300, byte-identical to the files `BSDS300/images/train/35010.jpg` and `train/97017.jpg` in `BSDS300-images.tgz` from https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Research/Projects/CS/vision/bsds/ (md5 2fee18e4acbe4b154a3b710b83c90938 and 30046a583edf11fea280861aa3fa6282; checked 2026-10-16). The same two files were distributed as `data/edge_detection/images/2.jpg` and `5.jpg` of Homework 1 (Fall 2026) and of the Fall 2025 homework. Citation requested by the dataset: D. Martin, C. Fowlkes, D. Tal and J. Malik, "A Database of Human Segmented Natural Images and its Application to Evaluating Segmentation Algorithms and Measuring Ecological Statistics", Proc. 8th Int. Conf. on Computer Vision, vol. 2, pp. 416-423, July 2001. Terms stated on the dataset page: "You are free to download a portion of the dataset for non-commercial research and educational purposes."
